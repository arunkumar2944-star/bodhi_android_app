# End-of-day attendance, versionCode 4

This update includes the existing stale-WORKING reconciliation fix and adds a calendar-day boundary at midnight Asia/Kolkata. It does not assume a shift ends at 6 pm. Overnight shifts require an explicit future policy.

Deploy CRM_TEST_Backend's matching change and apply migration 027 first. Configure the server-only CRON_SECRET and verify its midnight scheduler. The server records a separate SYSTEM_DAY_END policy checkout for an ended day with an accepted check-in and no checkout. This is not GPS-confirmed departure; the final duration remains unknown until reviewed. Accepted device and approved manager checkout supersede the fallback. An employee without a check-in never receives a manufactured attendance record.

The running Android service derives an end deadline from a validated server workday/time and elapsed realtime. Request-start mapping prevents response latency from extending the authorization. Refreshes can only shorten that deadline. The deadline stops tracking while offline as well as online; no phone wall-clock setting, unknown GPS reading or stale Room WORKING state can authorize a new workday. The app schedules network-only delivery and preserves original event UUIDs, timestamps and offline permits. No automatic morning tracking is added: open the app next workday for online reconciliation and fresh office verification.

Policy closure is performed by the server, so process termination and phone shutdown do not leave an old server session available to next-day events. Original-day late uploads are validated separately and do not reopen an ended day. A running service in RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED can now reach the existing state refresh instead of immediately exiting before recovery. Historical records with policy closure display Needs review and Day-end closure, with an explanation and unknown hours.

Validation: 70 portable JVM tests cover workday boundaries, missing/next-day authorization, stale-state reconciliation, pending writes, departure evidence, GPS, trusted time and existing engine behavior. The workflow compiles actual runtime model/refresh sources and uses its pinned Kotlin compiler's coroutine library.
